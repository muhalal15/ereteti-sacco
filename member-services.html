Member Services

Ereteti Dairy Sacco collects milk twice a day on three routes out of Ngong, records every delivery, and pays members once a month for the litres accepted.

Milk collection

Bring your churns to your collection centre for the morning and evening collections:

  • Kiserian route — from 6.30am and again from 4.00pm: Olepolos centre, Kandisi centre
  • Ngong route — from 6.00am and again from 3.30pm: Kibiko centre, Matasia centre, Oloolua centre
  • Rongai route — from 6.45am and again from 4.15pm: Nkaimurunya centre, Kware centre

See today's collections and all three routes

Delivery records and payment

Every delivery is recorded with the member number, the session and the litres, and whether it was accepted. Members are paid once a month for the accepted litres, at KSh 55 for each accepted litre.

See the deliveries record

Joining the sacco

Any dairy farmer within reach of one of our three routes may apply to join. You need your national ID number and a phone we can reach you on. Once your membership number is issued you may deliver from that day.
